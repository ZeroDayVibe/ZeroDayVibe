FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

A small FAQ section for visitors who want to understand the process before contacting ZeroDayVibe.

01

Do you need authorization?

Yes. Security testing should only be performed on systems where the owner has given clear permission and scope.

02

What do I receive?

Depending on the service, a report can include findings, evidence, risk context and remediation guidance.

03

Can you review APIs?

Yes. API security is one of the service areas presented on this site.

04

Can you work remotely?

Many security reviews can be coordinated remotely when the scope and access requirements allow it.

05

How do I start?

Use the contact form and describe the product, high-level scope and what you want to learn.

06

Do you publish client details?

Only publish project information that you are authorized to disclose. Sensitive client information should remain private.
